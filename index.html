A completely new browser engine built 100% in V

Vox focuses on performance and minimal resource usage. It will make your device feel snappy again.

Vox is a heavily modified translated fork of an old C++ Ladybird codebase from 2026.03.

Using Hacker News as a test (JS disabled): 15ms versus 60ms in Chrome, a quarter of the memory, and 40% less CPU.

Vox is still in alpha. Builds for Windows, macOS and Linux are available. If you want to contribute, join our Discord.
